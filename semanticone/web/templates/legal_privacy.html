Privacy Policy

How {{ settings.legal_entity }} handles personal and customer data in SemanticOne.

What we collect

  • Account data: name, email, hashed password, workspace membership.
  • Workspace content: semantic model documents and their version history.
  • Connection credentials: stored encrypted and used only to run your queries.
  • Usage data: the queries you run (request, generated SQL, row count, timing), used for metering, limits and support.
  • Billing data: handled by our payment processor; we store only customer and subscription identifiers.

What we don't collect

Query result rows are streamed back to you and are not stored.

How we use it

To provide and secure the service, meter usage, bill, and contact you about your account. We do not sell personal data.

Sub-processors

Hosting provider, payment processor and email provider, each under data processing terms.

Retention and deletion

Deleting a model or connection removes it immediately. To delete your account and workspace data, email {% if settings.support_email.endswith('@example.com') %}our contact form{% else %}{{ settings.support_email }}{% endif %}.
